# Experience Lab — Stage 3 development
This stage is a functional interactive research board, **not** a 360° model viewer or actual visual accessory fitting.
- Reuses the approved Area 33 white and red SVG identity.
- Four selectable research categories: optics, suppressors, lights, mounts. Selection, reset, inspector explanations, and copy of research questions work client-side.
- Explicit "not verified" states; no actual product records, inventory, legal eligibility claims, specifications or confidential dealer information.
- Visual presentation is abstract and does not purport to depict a real SKU.
- Next integration: exact model and accessory identifiers, evidence-reviewed relationship data, approved media rights and 360/3D model assets with accessible fallbacks. Public client must not infer compatibility from a graphic.
- Release gates: browser accessibility tests, physical/mobile visual review, backend data/tenant separation and privacy tests, media licensing, owner approval. Do not merge into main or deploy.
