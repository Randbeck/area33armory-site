# Area 33 Armory — Creative Direction (working brief)

**Status:** Direction for design prototypes, not an approved final logo or public launch.  
**Aesthetic:** Modern and confident, not tactical-template camouflage, busy gun-store graphics, or a generic distributor grid.

## Audience experience

Area 33 should look like a **technology-forward independent specialist**: strong editorial imagery, purposeful motion, immediate discovery, useful explanations and trustworthy facts. People can browse by interest or search precisely, understand products better, and know what is confirmed. Accessible contrast, keyboard navigation and fast phone performance are non-negotiable.

## Identity and visual directions

- Current brand: Area 33 Armory; line "Equipping Patriots • Supporting Heroes"; veteran-owned.
- Existing logo is a source asset to assess, **not automatically discarded**.
- Concept 1: Refined existing Area 33 identity.
- Concept 2: Ownable A33 monogram (A leg + interlocking pair of 3s).
- Concept 3: Custom AREA 33 wordmark with standalone 33 micro-icon.
- Explore solid black, off-white/white and restrained deep-red optional accent; a monochrome version must work.
- No visual shortcuts such as generic crosshairs, rifles, camouflage, stock storefront badges, or unlicensed product photography.

## Front page composition to prototype

1. Minimal global navigation and standout search entry.
2. Confident typographic hero, visually interesting but not overwhelming.
3. Editorial exploration paths with true categories.
4. A smart comparison/discovery module preview.
5. Product detail education and evidence explanation.
6. Short explanation of Area 33 services and veteran-owned values.
7. Clearly signposted upcoming Passport experience, without implying current launch.
8. Contact and business policies; no fake inventory, ratings or urgency.

## Product pages

Name, safe approved imagery, accurately classified type, sourced specifications, associated model relationships, uncertainty badges, comparison affordance, and action appropriate to actual readiness. Future approved customer-facing price and legitimate checkout may be attached without exposing supplier-cost data.

## Key implementation restrictions

- Site is not live commerce. Do not fake a functioning cart, real stock, verified prices or payment acceptance.
- Any external processor must support the actual business model and be approved before integration.
- Passport record viewing must be explicitly authorized, with no public individual-owner mapping.
- Keep Github Pages `main` unchanged until a reviewed launch.
