Better questions / Better decisions

Three things we believe in.

01 / IDENTITY

Compare the right item.

A model family is not an exact variant. Match the manufacturer, model and identifiers before comparing specifications.

02 / EVIDENCE

Know the source.

Published specifications need traceable sources, dates and clear handling of conflicting or missing details.

03 / CLARITY

Unknown means unknown.

Reference information is not proof of price or availability. We won't use invented certainty to fill gaps.
